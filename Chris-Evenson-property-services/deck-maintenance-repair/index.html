Deck Maintenance & Minor Repairs

Keep your outdoor living space looking its best.

Call (864) 381-8646

Your deck is an extension of your home. But exposure to sun, rain, and everyday use can leave it looking worn or needing attention.

I help homeowners maintain existing decks and address smaller, nonstructural repair needs before they become larger projects.

Deck services may include

  • Replacement of individual nonstructural deck boards
  • Minor surface and finish repairs
  • Refastening suitable loose trim or decking
  • Cleaning and surface preparation
  • General deck maintenance
  • Identifying visible concerns that may require further evaluation

Practical repairs and honest recommendations

Not every deck needs to be rebuilt. Sometimes targeted maintenance can extend the useful life of an existing outdoor space. If a deck has significant structural damage or safety concerns, I’ll recommend appropriate professional evaluation rather than treating a major issue as a minor repair.
