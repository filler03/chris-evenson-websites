What it is

Little things pile up. Let's knock them out.

Sometimes the little things around the house pile up. A loose cabinet handle, a sticking drawer, a door that needs adjusting, or hardware that needs tightening. These jobs may not justify hiring a large contractor, but they still need attention.

With the Honey-Do Hour, I'll come to your home and tackle as many qualifying small maintenance and repair tasks as reasonably possible during your appointment. Make a list, prioritize your tasks, and I'll work through them efficiently.

To keep things fair and clear, I can't promise that every single task will be finished within one hour — the goal is steady progress on the jobs that matter most to you, not a guarantee that your whole list fits the clock.
