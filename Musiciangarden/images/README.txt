PHOTOS FOLDER
=============
Save photos in this folder using these exact lowercase names. The site
finds them automatically; empty spots just show a placeholder note.

Photo slots on the site:
  images/hero.jpg    - hero photo (teaching scene, hands making music)
  images/chris.jpg   - photo of Chris (About Chris section)

MORE SLOTS (optional, add later):
  images/studio.jpg  - genuine teaching environment
  images/hands.jpg   - hands making music / close detail

TIPS
  - Use .jpg files with these exact names (convert PNG/HEIC to JPG first).
  - Download real photos from Google Photos via the three-dot menu >
    Download (NOT "Save page as", which saves a web page).
  - Aim for ~1200-1600 px on the long side, under ~500 KB each.
  - Warm, natural, authentic photos of Chris, instruments, hands making
    music, and real teaching settings. Quiet rather than flashy.
    Avoid literal gardening/plant imagery.
